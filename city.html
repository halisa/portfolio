MULTIMEDIA DESIGN INTERNSHIP ⛳

City of Kent Government ✦ 2024-2025

After 3 back-to-back semesters of studying abroad, I returned home to Washington state and walked the graduation stage. This City internship is my next adventure and I am learning lots about how local government works and the role that administration and communications plays. I drew inspiration from the idea of a winter candyland inside of a snow globe to design this year's Christmas Rush theme. For GIS Day, I incorporated a nostalgic Power Rangers themed look into the GIS Day poster and stickers.
