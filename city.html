City of Kent Government ✦ 2024-2025

After 3 back-to-back semesters of studying abroad, I returned home to Washington state and walked the graduation stage. This internship was my next adventure and I learned lots about how local government works and the role that administration and communications plays.

Christmas Rush

I drew inspiration from the idea of a winter candyland inside of a snow globe to design this year's Christmas Rush theme. At the beginning of my process, I played with the idea of a mascot which later evolved into purple and white snowglobe.

Cornucopia Days

The prompt for this year's Cornucopia design was "retro". At first I began making Y2K-inspired drafts, and later went further back in time to the 70s- and the cheerful walking corn character was created. I also had a lot of fun studying walking cycles and how to animate them!

Explore Kent Banners

These are street banners designed to encourage drivers/pedestrians/onlookers to see what Kent has to offer. I designed a set with one color palette and the other set are different colors, representing each unique aspect.
