Hilltop Artists ✦ 2021-2023

Hilltop Artists is a non-profit organization whose mission is to use "glass arts to connect young people from diverse cultural and economic backgrounds to better futures." I have worked on the designs of their internal documents, signage, social media posts, mailed postcards, and event branding.
