ARROW NONPROFIT POSTERS 🌊

Class Project ✦ 2022

Arrow is a nonprofit organization that advocates for Women's rights and health. Through a series of 3 posters, I tell a visual story weaving together intersectional causes such as climate change and reproductive justice.

PROCESS

Arrow does their work and activism through policy and education. Based in Malaysia, they are founded and ran by almost entirely women of color, of diverse ethnic and religious identities. I designed this series of poster as a response to constant tragedy, but also as a reminder for hope that many people and organizations are doing the work right now, and it is up to each of us to play our part as well. The content "The World Keeps Ending and the World Goes On" is from Franny Choi's poem.
