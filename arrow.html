Arrow Poster Trilogy

Arrow is a Women’s rights and health nonprofit organization based in Malaysia that advocates for intersectional causes such as climate change, disaster, etc. through policy and education. They are founded and ran by almost entirely women of color, of diverse ethnic and religious identities. I designed a poster series in the branding of this organization to call attention to the inequities Arrow is addressing.
